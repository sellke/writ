# Writ issue

Read `../writ/rules.md` first.

Capture it. Do not solve it. The person is in the middle of something else.

Write `.writ/issues/<date>-<name>.md` with:

- a title a stranger would understand;
- **What:** the bug or idea in two or three sentences, and how to see it if it is a bug;
- **Why it matters:** who it affects and how much;
- **Where:** the part of the product, in plain words;
- **Size:** a guess at small, medium, or large.

Fill in what the conversation and a quick look already tell you. Ask at most one question, and only if the title would otherwise be wrong. If it duplicates an open issue, add to that one.

Say where you saved it, and go back to what you were doing.

If the person has asked for issues to go to a connected tracker, file it there and note the link in your reply.
