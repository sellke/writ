A viewer for one /prototype exploration record.
Who uses it: a developer who returns weeks later to learn why a prototype went the direction it did.
A record holds two or three drafts. Each draft is a rendered page or a short code sketch, plus what it committed to.
The viewer shows the drafts side by side, which draft was chosen, and one line on why each other draft lost.
The developer must be able to compare the drafts and find the reason for the choice in under a minute.
