A page that shows how far one Writ spec has progressed.
Who uses it: a maintainer who checks a spec several times a day while its stories are being implemented.
A spec has three to eight stories. Each story has a status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, Blocked) and three to eight acceptance criteria.
For each story the page shows its status and which acceptance criteria have a passing test and which do not.
The maintainer must be able to tell what is left on the spec without opening any story file.
